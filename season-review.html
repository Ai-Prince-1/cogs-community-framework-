Season Review

The one-page Season Summary.

The written record the Scribe produces after the Season Retrospective in Week 12. It becomes part of the Circle's permanent record, so the next Season starts from what was learned and not from memory. See Handbook §3.

When

Within a week of the Retrospective, posted to the group thread and kept with the Circle's records.

Template

``` SEASON SUMMARY: Season __ (dates ____ to ____) Circle: ____ Scribe: ____

  1. Domains we focused on - ____

  2. Key learnings (3–5 bullets, each with evidence) - ____

  3. Commitments - Kept: __ of __ (approximate is fine) - Patterns behind the ones we missed: ____

  4. Health check (from the Retrospective) - More open or more closed than 12 weeks ago? ____ - Who spoke least, and what we'll change: ____ - Any warning patterns from the Manifesto? ____

  5. One improvement for next Season - ____

  6. Roles for next Season - Facilitator order: ____ - Level 2 (if active): Steward ____ Scribe ____ Bridge ____

  7. Carry forward / leave behind - Carry: ____ - Leave: ____ ```

Guidance

  • Keep it to one page. Brevity means it actually gets read next Season.
  • Record what happened, not who to blame.
  • Every member may read it; anyone may propose a correction.
