Brand Assets

The logo, what it means and how to use it.

File Use
COGS_Community_Framework_Logo_v2.jpg Primary logo, light backgrounds
COGS_Community_Framework_Logo_Dark_Mode.jpg Dark backgrounds

The logo, like the rest of the framework, is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Circles may use it freely; please don't alter it in ways that imply a different organization.

What the mark means

The logo translates the COGS Framework (Circle of Good Samaritans) into a single mark.

  • Three interlocking cogs. Mechanical advantage through collective action, "moving together what none can move alone." The three gears also hint at the three tiers: Circle, Mesh and Assembly.
  • The star at the center. The negative space between the gears forms a star: the individual purpose and flourishing that a supportive Circle makes possible.
  • The infinity-loop wordmark. In "COGS", the "CO" forms a subtle infinity loop. It stands for the self-renewing network: a person supported by a Circle gains capacity, then joins or seeds a new Circle. It also reflects that the framework is un-ownable and permanently forkable.

Colors

Color Meaning
Teal / petrol blue Trust, reliability, systems design
Coral / orange Action, vitality, the human warmth of community

Both colors are chosen for high contrast in light and dark modes.

Typography

A strong, clean sans-serif for the wordmark and headings, kept simple and modern.
