Meetings

Season Opening

Week 1: choose domains, set intentions.

Week 1 of every 12-week Season. It's the one gathering allowed to run longer than 70 minutes, because it sets everything that follows. Full guidance is in Handbook §3.

Agenda (90–120 minutes)

Item Time Purpose
Check-in round 15 min How is everyone arriving into this new Season?
Domain selection 20 min Vote on 2–3 life domains to focus on this Season.
Personal intentions 20 min Each member states one personal intention for the Season: a direction, not a goal.
Role assignments 15 min Set the Facilitator order for the coming weeks. At Level 2, also assign Steward, Scribe and Bridge.
Resource conversation 10 min Does the Circle want to pool anything this Season? If yes, Level 2 Module B activates.
Outward support (optional) 10 min Does the Circle have capacity to support someone outside the group this Season?
Close 10 min The Scribe reads back all intentions and commitments. The first weekly Facilitator is confirmed.

Record to keep (Scribe)

Season: ____ Dates: ____ to ____ Domains: 1. ____ 2. ____ 3. ____ Intentions: - [member]: ____ Facilitator order: ____ Level 2 roles (if any): Steward ____ Scribe ____ Bridge ____ Pooled resources (if any): ____ Outward support (if any): ____

Before you leave

  • [ ] Everyone has said their intention out loud
  • [ ] Facilitator order is written down and shared in the thread
  • [ ] Mid-Season Check-In (Week 6) and Retrospective (Week 12) are on the calendar
