# Weekly Gathering

The heartbeat of every Circle: the same 70-minute structure every week. The structure is what creates the safety for honest conversation. When people know what to expect, they can relax into being present. Full guidance is in [Handbook §2](../docs/handbook.md).

## Agenda (70 minutes, locked)

| Time | Phase | What happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10 min** | **The Open** | Check-in round. One sentence each: *how are you arriving today?* No advice, no responses. Just listening. |
| **45 min** | **The Share** | Each member reports on last week's commitment (done, not done, or partly done) and on the Season's domains. What did you try? What worked? What did you learn? |
| **15 min** | **The Commit** | Each member names one specific, self-chosen action for the coming week. Concrete. Measurable. Achievable by next gathering. |
| **Close** | **The Hand-Off** | The current Facilitator names next week's Facilitator. End on time. |

## After the gathering (same hour if possible)

Each member posts their commitment to the group thread, the **Zero-Overhead Ledger**:

> **Domain:** [e.g. Financial Literacy]
> **My action this week:** [one specific action and when it will be done]

## Facilitator checklist

- [ ] Start and end on time
- [ ] Equal time for everyone in The Share
- [ ] No advice-giving during The Open
- [ ] Push back gently on vague commitments
- [ ] Name the next Facilitator before closing

## If someone misses

1. They read the thread to catch up and post their commitment as normal.
2. After three missed gatherings without word, the Facilitator checks in privately before the next session.

## Online gatherings

Cameras on for the full 70 minutes, a quiet setting, and extra care with The Open. Remotely it does the work the room would otherwise do.
