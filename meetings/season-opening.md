# Season Opening

Week 1 of every 12-week Season. It's the one gathering allowed to run longer than 70 minutes, because it sets everything that follows. Full guidance is in [Handbook §3](../docs/handbook.md).

## Agenda (90–120 minutes)

| Item | Time | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Check-in round** | 15 min | How is everyone arriving into this new Season? |
| **Domain selection** | 20 min | Vote on 2–3 life domains to focus on this Season. |
| **Personal intentions** | 20 min | Each member states one personal intention for the Season: a direction, not a goal. |
| **Role assignments** | 15 min | Set the Facilitator order for the coming weeks. At Level 2, also assign [Steward](../roles/steward.md), [Scribe](../roles/scribe.md) and [Bridge](../roles/bridge.md). |
| **Resource conversation** | 10 min | Does the Circle want to pool anything this Season? If yes, Level 2 Module B activates. |
| **Outward support** *(optional)* | 10 min | Does the Circle have capacity to support someone outside the group this Season? |
| **Close** | 10 min | The Scribe reads back all intentions and commitments. The first weekly Facilitator is confirmed. |
