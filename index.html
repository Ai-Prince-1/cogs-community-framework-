We move together what none of us can move alone.

COGS is an open framework for small groups of 3 to 7 people who meet every week to help each other grow physically, financially, emotionally and relationally. No doctrine, no permanent leaders, nothing to buy. Anyone can start a Circle tonight.

Start a CircleRead the manifesto

Small by design, able to grow

Circle3 to 7 people who meet weekly. The core unit, where trust lives.
MeshUp to 7 Circles, about 49 people, sharing what they learn.
AssemblyUp to 3 Meshes, about 147 people. Meets once a year.

Start in five steps

  1. Find your people. Three to seven adults you would show up for every week.
  2. Read the manifesto together and decide whether it fits you.
  3. Hold a Season Opening. Choose two or three life domains and say one intention each.
  4. Meet weekly for twelve weeks using the 70-minute gathering. Rotate who facilitates.
  5. Close with a Retrospective, then decide what to keep and what to change.

What keeps it healthy

Leadership rotates
Everyone facilitates before anyone facilitates twice. There are no gurus.
Leaving is free
No shame, no social debt. The door is open in both directions.
Money stays visible
If a Circle pools anything, one rotating Steward keeps a ledger every member can read.
Anyone can fork it
If your community needs something different, take the framework and change it.
